# Tripcraft MVP Image Asset Plan

## Goal

Create a small, coherent image library for the India-first visual identity without commissioning one image per state, city, itinerary activity, or weather state. The approved style is editorial vector/cut-paper illustration with warm paper texture, evergreen ink, restrained saffron/coral/teal accents, and no text embedded in the artwork.

This is an asset plan only. No images are generated by this document.

## Current image inventory

- The earlier six-city image/preset direction has been removed from the planner and image guide. Do not use old city-specific banners or `CuratedCityScene` entries as approved assets. Any legacy files/components still present in the repository are not part of this asset plan. The new image set uses broad regional art by default, plus only the 11 landmark-specific exceptions listed below.
- The destination resolver can currently surface external destination imagery. Once approved local artwork exists for an Indian region, that local illustration should be the predictable visual choice for its cities rather than an unrelated photo.
- Weather is currently represented with code-native icons. The new weather illustrations below are shared across the product.
- The dashboard’s featured trip, trip workspace, and trips-library card should all use the same resolved city/region artwork with different crops. **Do not generate separate copies for each page.** For an empty dashboard, reuse a crop of the landing illustration rather than creating a dashboard-only asset.
- The Google Map is a real product surface, not artwork: do not generate a map, route, pin field, or map background.
- Itinerary activity cards remain text-only. Do not generate venue/activity thumbnails.
- The two MVP page mockups in `design-references/` are layout/style references, not production artwork to crop or ship.

## Image count

**28 new master images are planned:** 1 landing illustration, 4 weather illustrations, 11 India regional destination illustrations, 11 selective landmark illustrations, and 1 global fallback illustration. The regional scenes cover all of India; a small landmark tier adds distinct art only for destinations with especially recognizable identity. This is an artwork-resolution choice, not a return of planner presets or a one-image-per-city system.

Dashboard, workspace, and trip-card crops do not add to the count because they reuse the same destination master.

## Keep landmark exceptions deliberately bounded

The old six-city planner/preset tier remains removed. The 11 landmark images above are a narrowly selected visual exception only; the regional scenes remain the default for all other destinations. Do not turn this list into planner quick picks or add more city overrides without Japjit’s approval. Keep destination identity and geographic resolution driven by existing trusted metadata, and do not change supported destinations as part of image generation.

## Art and crop requirements

- Destination/region masters: landscape 3:2 source, season-neutral, no text or baked-in UI, with a strong but not edge-clipped focal subject and enough quiet space for responsive crops. Use the same master for the workspace banner, dashboard featured trip, and trips-library card; store/use one focal point per image if needed.
- Landing hero: wide landscape artwork with its visual focus biased to the right so the real webpage can retain live text and controls on the left. It is a standalone illustration asset, not a screenshot of the generated landing mockup.
- Weather art: transparent square PNGs, isolated silhouette, readable at both small and large sizes.
- Keep the illustration style consistent across assets: expressive layered vector/cut-paper shapes, controlled paper grain, and the approved palette. No photorealism, flags, typographic labels, fake map details, activity thumbnails, or watermarks.
- Use the same scene asset for all crops. Do not create separate “dashboard version,” “workspace version,” and “trip-card version” of a city image.

## Generation order and approval gate

1. Generate a style-proof batch: landing hero, Jaipur landmark art, one Northeast regional master, and all four weather assets. Review the artwork in the real page crops before approving the rest.
2. If the proof passes, generate the remaining ten landmark masters, the remaining ten regional masters, and the one global fallback.
3. Integrate assets after approval, then test focal crops on landing, My Home, workspace, trips library, and weather summary at desktop and mobile sizes. Keep the map and activity cards image-free as specified above.

The image pack is complete when the 11 selected destinations resolve to their landmark art, all other Indian destinations resolve locally to the correct regional illustration, arbitrary non-India destinations have a neutral fallback, all six weather states resolve through the four shared weather assets/compositions, and no decorative map or per-activity image has been introduced.
